# Rating Responses

Source: https://docs.smartchat.ai.t-systems.net/user/guide/feedback/

> Rate a response, share it with the owners of your Space, and withdraw your consent.

Below each response you can rate it with **Good Response** (thumbs up) or **Bad Response** (thumbs down). The owners of your Space use these ratings to see where Smartchat helps and where it falls short.

> **Note**
>
> The rating buttons only appear when feedback is switched on for your Space. Space Owners can turn it off.

## [Rating a response](https://docs.smartchat.ai.t-systems.net/user/guide/feedback/#rating-a-response)

1. Click **Good Response** or **Bad Response** below the response.
2. Pick what you liked or what was wrong. A reason is required when you rate a response as bad.
3. Add a **Comment** if you like and click **Submit**.

The first time you rate a response, Smartchat asks for your consent before anything is shared. Click **Agree and submit** to share your rating.

## [What is shared](https://docs.smartchat.ai.t-systems.net/user/guide/feedback/#what-is-shared)

The Space Owners of your Space and the Application Owners see:

- your rating and comment,
- the chat up to the rated response,
- your email address.

Your feedback is kept until it is deleted. If your instance deletes feedback automatically after a number of days, the rating card tells you so. To remove a single rating, open it again and click **Delete feedback**.

## [Withdrawing your consent](https://docs.smartchat.ai.t-systems.net/user/guide/feedback/#withdrawing-your-consent)

Click **Revoke consent** in your settings under **General**, or in the rating card. This deletes all feedback you have shared, in every Space: every rating, category and comment. Space Owners and Application Owners can no longer see any of it, and the deletion cannot be undone.

The next time you rate a response, you are asked for your consent again.
