# Registration & Login

Source: https://docs.smartchat.ai.t-systems.net/user/guide/getting-started/

> Registration and login for Smartchat.

## [Registration & Login](https://docs.smartchat.ai.t-systems.net/user/guide/getting-started/#registration--login)

If available, please log in using your company’s **Single Sign-On (SSO)**. Otherwise, follow the instructions below.

### [Registration](https://docs.smartchat.ai.t-systems.net/user/guide/getting-started/#registration)

Your account is created when an Application Owner or the Owner of your Space [adds you as a member](https://docs.smartchat.ai.t-systems.net/user/admin/managing-space-members/). You will receive an email with a verification link. Please check your email and use the “Forgot Password” option to create a new password. Your **new password** must contain **at least 16 characters**.

*\[Image: Registration confirmation email]*

*\[Image: Password update page]*

### [Login](https://docs.smartchat.ai.t-systems.net/user/guide/getting-started/#login)

Open the login link provided in the welcome email you received. Then log in with your email address and password, or use your company SSO.

*\[Image: Login page]*

Depending on your installation, you may also be asked for a code from an authenticator app. See [Two-Factor Authentication](https://docs.smartchat.ai.t-systems.net/user/concepts/two-factor-authentication/).
