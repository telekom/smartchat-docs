# Reviewing Feedback

Source: https://docs.smartchat.ai.t-systems.net/user/admin/feedback/

> How Space Owners review the ratings members give on responses, and how to switch feedback on or off for a Space.

Members can [rate responses](https://docs.smartchat.ai.t-systems.net/user/guide/feedback/) and, with their consent, share them with you. Space Owners and Application Owners review this feedback in [Administration](https://docs.smartchat.ai.t-systems.net/user/admin/).

The **Feedback** page appears only when feedback is available for your Smartchat environment.

## [Opening the feedback](https://docs.smartchat.ai.t-systems.net/user/admin/feedback/#opening-the-feedback)

Open **Administration → Space Owner**, pick the Space in **Selected Space** and click **Feedback** under **Manage**.

The list shows the feedback given in this Space with its **Time**, **Sender**, **Model**, rating (**Positive** or **Negative**), **Category** and **Status**. Filter by rating, category, model, status and date, or use **Search comments and chats**. **Open feedback** shows the comment together with the chat up to the rated response.

## [Working through feedback](https://docs.smartchat.ai.t-systems.net/user/admin/feedback/#working-through-feedback)

- **Mark as read** and **Mark as unread** keep track of what you have looked at.
- **Delete** removes feedback for good. Select several items to mark or delete them together.

Feedback disappears when the member who gave it [withdraws their consent](https://docs.smartchat.ai.t-systems.net/user/guide/feedback/#withdrawing-your-consent). If your environment deletes feedback after a number of days, the page tells you so.

## [Switching feedback on or off](https://docs.smartchat.ai.t-systems.net/user/admin/feedback/#switching-feedback-on-or-off)

The **Feedback** switch on the [Space](https://docs.smartchat.ai.t-systems.net/user/admin/space-settings/#feedback) settings page decides whether the members of this Space see the rating buttons. Turning it off hides them; feedback given before stays on the **Feedback** page.
